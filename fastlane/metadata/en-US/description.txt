Pigeon is a quick-capture inbox for you and your AI agents.

Press ⌘⇧Space anywhere, type a thought, and get back to work. Pigeon saves it as a task line in a plain Markdown file, in a folder you choose. Open your tasks in any editor and sync them however you like.

WORK WITH YOUR AI AGENTS
Turn on the optional Pigeon Cloud and connect Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client. Tag a task for:agent and your agent picks it up. When it needs you to review, approve or send something, it adds a task for you, so the next step lands in your inbox. It works with the AI subscription you already have.

CAPTURE IN A SECOND
• A Spotlight-style panel from anywhere with a global shortcut
• Priorities (!1–!3), @projects and #tags as you type
• Natural dates: due:tomorrow, due:next friday, defer:in 3 days
• Autocomplete for your tags and projects

LIGHT TRIAGE, NOT A TASK MANAGER
• See today's tasks from the menu bar
• Check off, edit or delete, with undo
• Jump between days with a calendar

YOUR DATA, YOUR FILES
• Tasks are plain Markdown lines you own
• Pigeon Cloud is off until you connect it, and you can delete your cloud account and data at any time
• No tracking, no ads
